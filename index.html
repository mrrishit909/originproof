OriginProof reads a single shipment record, the supplier links someone declared, and the documents attached to each supplier. It then checks three fictional evidence rules and shows which record each finding points at. It does not decide anything about the shipment.

    Boundaries

    What this is not

    • Not customs law, a rule of origin, or a tariff or trade-agreement check.
    • No sanctions or denied-party screening.
    • No geography. AA and BB are opaque placeholder labels, and nothing is inferred from supplier location or routing.
    • No clearance decision. The only statuses are FINDINGS_PRESENT and NO_FINDINGS, and the second means only that three fictional rules produced nothing.
    • One shipment per input. Supplier links are taken as declared, never discovered or scored.
    • The report fingerprint is an unkeyed hash that catches accidental drift. It is not a signature.
    • Qualified review of real policy would have to come before any real-world use. The full list is in the repository’s docs/LIMITATIONS.md.
